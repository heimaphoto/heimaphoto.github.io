## 外拍活动NO.014 | 苏州东山荷花节抬猛将之中篇

当苏州东山各村的猛将齐聚葑山寺时，广场上的欢庆表演愈加热闹。那时我是有机会上岸的。虽然船被挤在河道边缘，但如果经过紧挨着的船只慢慢挪过去，想办法跳上岸，倒也未必不行。

不过朋友提醒我，上岸以后，回程时仍然要找到同村的船，否则如果上错了船，就会被带到其他村里去了。

最终我们还是选择留在船上。既然如此，就拍好眼前的风景吧。这大概也是一种取舍。

终于快要回程了。天色已经全亮，这一篇拍下的照片也更多一些。:)

### Carrying the General Parade in Dongshan, Suzhou — Part II

As the Generals from villages across Dongshan gathered at Fengshan Temple, the celebrations in the square grew livelier. I did have a chance to go ashore. Although our boat was squeezed against the edge of the waterway, I probably could have made my way across the neighboring boats and found a way onto land.

A friend reminded me, however, that I would still have to find the boat from our own village for the return journey. If I boarded the wrong one, I might end up being taken back to another village.

In the end, we decided to stay on the boat. Since that was the choice, I might as well concentrate on photographing what was in front of me. Sometimes photography is also about choosing what to let go.

At last, it was almost time to head back. By then the sky was fully bright, and I ended up taking more photographs for this part of the story. :)
