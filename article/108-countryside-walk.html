秋日阴天，乡村随拍

2026.09.26 · 摄影笔记 · 南河港 & 仁巷·苏州。 · Rx1RM2

有时只是路过，临时下车走走，也会遇见意外的风景。

Sometimes, all it takes is a passing drive and a spontaneous walk to discover an unexpected landscape.

那天开车经过南河港时，正好还有点时间，我临时决定下车走走。

我童年时在乡村生活过，乡村的景象对我一直有着天然的吸引力。

如今江南的乡村变化很大，有时偶然开车经过乡村，眼前的一些景象还是会让我忍不住停下来看看。那些熟悉又陌生的田野、道路、房屋和树木，总会让我由衷地感叹：现在的乡村，竟然这么美了吗？

ENGLISH

That day, I was driving past Nanhegang when I happened to have some time to spare, so I decided to stop and take a walk.

I spent part of my childhood in the countryside, and rural landscapes have always held a natural attraction for me.

The countryside in Jiangnan has changed greatly over the years. Even now, when I happen to drive through the countryside, certain scenes still make me want to stop and take a closer look. The fields, roads, houses, and trees feel both familiar and unfamiliar, and I often find myself thinking: How did the countryside become this beautiful?
