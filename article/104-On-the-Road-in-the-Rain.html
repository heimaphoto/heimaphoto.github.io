雨天在路上

2026.09.18 · 摄影笔记 · iPhone-Air · DAZZ-CAM ::: S Classic

雨天在路上，

公路一直向前延伸。

路、人和陌生的风景，

都被雨水写成朦胧的诗。

ENGLISH

On the Road in the Rain

On a rainy day, the road keeps stretching ahead.
Roads, people, and unfamiliar scenes are rewritten by the rain into a hazy poem.
