外拍活动NO.016 | 水场-一杯咖啡的休闲时光

2026.09.29 · 摄影笔记 · 水场Jolifornia·青浦·上海 · Rx1RM2

如果把这次外拍活动分成上午场和下午场，那么在“水场”的这段时间，大概就是中场休息。旧水厂被改造成了一个临湖的休闲空间，喝咖啡、聊天、发发呆，也顺手拍下一些建筑、光影和湖边的人。

If this photo outing were divided into a morning session and an afternoon session, our time at “Shuichang” would be the intermission. A former water plant has been turned into a relaxed space by Dianshan Lake, where we could have coffee, chat, slow down, and casually photograph the old structures, shifting light, and people by the lake.

这次外拍活动如果分为上午场、下午场，那么这篇就应该是中场休息。

上午拍摄结束后，能在这样一个悠闲的地方放松下来，暂时忘掉所有事情。偶尔闲聊几句，或者小睡一会儿，又或者拿起相机随手拍几张小品，也是件幸事。

ENGLISH

If this photo outing were divided into a morning session and an afternoon session, this would be the intermission.
After a morning of shooting, it was a pleasure to slow down in a place like this and simply relax for a while. We chatted occasionally, took a short nap, or picked up our cameras and made a few quiet photographs. Sometimes, that is enough.
